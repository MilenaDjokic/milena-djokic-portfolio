D Beograđanka is a furniture restoration brand based in Belgrade, Serbia. Each piece is one of a kind, carrying its own history and character. The website design is intentionally clean and minimal, allowing the furniture to take center stage while reflecting the brand’s refined, thoughtful approach to craftsmanship.

The challenge behind D Beograđanka was to create an e-commerce experience that felt less transactional and more like stepping into an art gallery. The brand's philosophy is rooted in craftsmanship, uniqueness, and storytelling, so the digital experience was designed to slow users down and encourage exploration. Through immersive imagery, generous whitespace, refined typography, and subtle motion, the interface balances commercial goals with emotional engagement. The result is a website that not only showcases products but also communicates the value behind each restored piece, allowing users to connect with the brand's artistic

Featured pieces on the homepage serve as an entry point into the brand’s products. Rather than displaying a large catalog, the homepage focuses on a carefully curated selection of restored furniture. Red pieces were deliberately featured to introduce contrast, guide the user’s eye, and add character to an otherwise neutral and minimal interface.

Rather than a dense listing, the grid catalog presents furniture as individual objects within a cohesive visual system. The layout encourages exploration of brand’s unique aesthetic. The single product view gives each piece space to breathe, combining detailed photography with minimal text. This approach supports informed decision-making while reinforcing the uniqueness of every restored item.

Category filters allow users to quickly narrow the catalog by furniture type, while the search bar supports direct access to specific pieces. Both features are designed to enhance usability without disrupting the clean, gallery-like browsing experience.
